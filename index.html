How to measure your torso

01Find your C7 vertebra — the bony bump at the base of your neck where it meets your shoulders.
02Place your hands on your hips, thumbs pointing back. Find the iliac crest — the top of your hip bones.
03Measure from C7 down your spine to the iliac crest. Record in inches or centimetres — FitMyPack accepts both.
04For waist: measure around your torso at belly button level — where the hip belt will sit. Record in inches or centimetres.
